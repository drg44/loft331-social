## Wink and photo-match rules (Dave 2026-09-21, after "Day three at home. The cat runs the meetings." on a bed-laptop photo)
- **The photo must literally show what the headline says.** Cat joke → a cat in the photo. Mute joke → a video-call screen. Laundry → laundry. If the pool has no photo that shows the thing, do NOT swap in a vague "home office" shot — go get one (below). A headline/photo mismatch is a hard fail; re-do before showing Dave.
- **Read `references/wink-humour.md` before writing any wink.** Adapt a proven premise from that list; never invent one.
- **Wink test, all three must pass before rendering:** (1) the situation is something the reader has actually lived this month; (2) say the two lines out loud — they sound like a person, not a caption; (3) a stranger gets it on the first read with no setup. "Day three at home. The cat runs the meetings." fails (2) and (3). "The cat's in the meeting. Again. Nobody minded." passes.
- **Testimonial headline must stand alone as a complete, natural sentence** — no clause fragments ("A day pass at the Loft, and I was set."). If the strongest sentence doesn't stand alone, pick a different sentence or a different review.
- **Getting a matching photo when the pool has none:** run `python3 ~/loft331-mgmt/brand/social/find_stock.py "<what the photo must show>"` — it searches Openverse + Wikimedia Commons for CC0 / CC-BY / CC-BY-SA photos ≥ 1600 px wide, downloads the top candidates to a contact sheet for you to pick from, and `--pick N --name stock-<slug>.jpg` saves it into Drive → Photos/stock and appends the credit (title, author, licence, source URL) to `Photos/stock/CREDITS.csv`. CC-BY / BY-SA photos need the credit line in the caption's last line ("Photo: <author>, CC BY 2.0"); CC0 needs none. Never use "all rights reserved" or unknown-licence images. If nothing usable comes back, tell Dave what you need instead of forcing a mismatch.
