# Loft 331 Workstudio — Brand Guidelines

**Source of truth for all Loft 331 content, calendars, and visuals.**
Every value below was measured on the live website, loft331.ca, in September 2026. Use these exact values. Do not substitute, approximate, or invent colours, fonts, or spacing.

---

## 1. How to use this document

Whenever you create Loft 331 content (content calendars, social posts, captions, ads, or visuals):

1. Follow the **colour** and **typography** rules in this document exactly. Use the **voice** rules as the default; a writing tone given in the user's prompt overrides the default voice (Section 6).
2. **Every post names its visual** (see Sections 8–9): a real Loft 331 photo file or the green card, plus a style. Images are rendered outside NotebookLM from those fields; no image prompts are needed.
3. **Follow the weekly structure in Section 10** and the calendar format in Section 11.

- **Body text is slate (#334155), never green.**

- **Do not introduce any other colours:** no bright blues, oranges, purples, reds, or neon.
- **Social graphics never use a plain white background.** Use either a photo or a solid #013C3F green background (optionally a subtle gradient to #1A5C44).

## 4. Typography (exact live-site values)

### Fonts

| Role | Font | Weight | Where to get it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Display / headings** | **Fraunces** (a soft, high-contrast modern serif) | **600 (SemiBold)** | Google Fonts |
| **Body / UI / buttons** | **Lato** (a clean humanist sans-serif) | **400 (Regular)**; **500 (Medium)** for buttons and feature labels | Google Fonts |

No other fonts. No script, handwritten, condensed, or decorative typefaces.

### Letter spacing (tracking)

| Text type | Letter spacing | Example on site |
|---|---|---|
| **All headings (Fraunces)** | **−0.065em** (tight) | 28px heading = −1.82px |
| Body paragraphs (Lato) | **Normal (0)** | — |
| Text links / "Learn More" (Lato 500) | **−0.025em** | 16px = −0.4px |
| Small uppercase labels / eyebrows (Lato 400) | **+0.05em** (wide), **UPPERCASE** | "SEE IT FOR YOURSELF", "331 ELMWOOD DRIVE" |
| Buttons (Lato 500) | Normal (0), sentence case | "Book a Tour" |

### Line height

| Text type | Line height |
|---|---|
| Headings | **1.0** (tight; each line sits close to the next) |
| Short sub-headlines | 1.1–1.35 |
| Body paragraphs | **1.5** (up to 1.75 for long reading) |

### Type sizes (live site)

- Hero headline: scales from about 34px (mobile) up to **83px** (desktop), Fraunces 600, white on photo
- Section headings: **24–30px**, Fraunces 600, #013C3F
- Body: **16px**, Lato 400, #334155
- Small labels: **12–14px**, Lato 400, uppercase, +0.05em, #64748B (or white at 70% on dark backgrounds)

### Typography rules

- Headlines are **sentence case**, never ALL CAPS.
- **Each sentence of a headline goes on its own line.** A sentence never wraps mid-line. If it's too long, rewrite it shorter; don't shrink the text.
- Headlines are **left-aligned**.

## 6. Voice and messaging

**Tone precedence:** if the user's prompt specifies a writing tone (for example "persuasive", "conversational", "inspirational"), **that tone takes priority** over the default voice below. Apply it to the copy. Everything else in this section still holds regardless of tone: real facts and prices only, Canadian spelling, caption structure, hashtag limits, and the headline rules. When the prompt names no tone, use the default voice below.

- **Warm, confident, plain-spoken.** Short sentences.
- **Two-beat headlines are the house style:** "Great coworking. Even better people." / "The space will draw you in. The people will keep you here." / "Work better. Together."
- **Community first.** Lead with people, then amenities.
- **Humour is dry and understated.** One wink per post at most, in passing: "…and where the espresso lives." Never a meme, never sarcasm at a customer's expense, never a joke that needs an explanation.
- **Concrete over hype.** Use real numbers from the source documents only. Never invent prices or facts.
- **Friction-removal framing:** "No meters, no garages, no circling the block. Just pull in and get to work."
- **Canadian spelling** (colour, centre, neighbour).
- **Never:** corporate buzzwords ("synergy", "hustle"), stacked exclamation marks, more than 2 emoji per caption, or fake urgency.
- **Captions:** 1–3 short paragraphs, hook in the first line, one idea per post, one call to action ("Book a tour at loft331.ca" or "Call/text 506 850 5358"), and 3–5 hashtags on the last line.
- **Hashtag pool:** #Moncton #MonctonNB #coworking #Loft331 #workstudio #smallbusinessNB #MonctonEvents

### Who we write for (from 100+ five-star reviews)

Write every post for one of these people. They are the members we have and the ones we want more of:

- **The independent professional** — realtor, consultant, self-employed owner. Needs a professional place to meet clients and a calm home base. Says: "perfect environment when I need a professional office or a comfortable spot to meet with clients."
- **The deep-focus worker** — developer, analyst, writer, remote employee. Wants quiet, fast Wi-Fi, no interruptions. Says: "ideal for deep, uninterrupted work," "designed for focus."
- **The connector** — small-business owner who comes for the people. Says: "met three great new connections," "great energy, great people, an awesome place to network."
- **The visitor between meetings** — in town for a day, needs a desk now. Says: "was in town and needed a place to get some work done."
- **The well-travelled sceptic** — has used WeWork-class spaces elsewhere and judges by execution. Says: "up there with the best," "polished, energetic… without feeling too corporate or cold."

### Their words, not ours

Use the language members already use to describe us. Lead with these, in this order of how often members mention them:

1. **The people** — "nice people," "great energy, great people," "Dan & Caroline were fantastic," "the people who already call it home."
2. **The coffee** — "amazing coffee," "excellent espresso," "unlimited coffee." Coffee is a feature here, not a footnote.
3. **Fast Wi-Fi** — "fast and reliable," "stress-free."
4. **Quiet and focus** — "deep, uninterrupted work," "calm," "relaxed yet productive."
5. **The design and light** — "beautifully designed," "polished," "natural light," "comfortable furniture."
6. **Easy parking** — "easy parking," "plenty of parking," "easy commute."
7. **Connections** — "met new connections," "grew my network."

The sentence that sums up the brand, written by a member: **"polished, energetic atmosphere that makes you want to get things done, but without feeling too corporate or cold."** Every post should be true to that sentence.

**Never mention:** a gym (we no longer have one), "sophisticated," "excellence," "premium," "ambitious professionals," or any corporate-brochure phrasing. Members don't talk that way, so neither do we.

## 9. Visuals — required with every content calendar

Images are rendered from the calendar by Loft 331's own template (real photo or green card, real logo, real fonts). The calendar only needs to supply, per post: **Headline, Subline, Photo File, Style.** The rules below describe what that template produces, so the copy is written to fit it.

Every Loft 331 visual must follow these rules:

1. **Size:** square 1080×1080 for feed posts; 1080×1920 for Stories/Reels; 1200×630 for link previews.
2. **Background, one of two:**
   - **Real Loft 331 photo:** the photo source named in the calendar's Photo File column (Section 8 describes each one). Show that photo as the full background, exactly as it appears in the source, with a soft dark gradient at the top and bottom as in `reference-layout.png`. Never invent a different scene.
   - **Stock photo under the green wash** (Section 8 stock list) for subjects that aren't the space.
   - **Brand gradient** (`loft_gradient.png`) only as a last resort when neither a Loft 331 photo nor a listed stock photo fits.
   - **Green style (2 posts per week):** the same photo and layout, but the whole photo is washed in deep teal green #013C3F (about 75% opacity, slightly heavier at top and bottom). Used only when the calendar's Style column says `green`.
   - Never a plain white background.
3. **Logo:** reproduce the logo exactly as it appears in the source `Loft331-Logo-White.png`, in the **top-left corner** (Section 7).
4. **Headline:** Fraunces SemiBold (600), letter spacing −0.065em, line height 1.0, white, left-aligned, sentence case. The headline block sits in the lower-left, starting just below the vertical centre (as in `reference-layout.png`). Maximum 2 lines, about 26 characters per line, one sentence per line.
5. **Subline:** Lato Regular, white, directly below the headline. **Say-it-out-loud test:** plain speech only, something you'd say to a person across a table. No personification, no writer-cute lines ("your back files a complaint" fails; "then it's just a couch again" passes). **Maximum 2 lines, 55 characters per line.** Every post has one; it comes from the calendar's Subline column, never from the caption.
6. **Website:** "LOFT331.CA" small, uppercase, letter spacing +0.05em, Lato, white, bottom-left.
7. **Margins:** about 10% on the left and right.
8. **No extras:** no borders, lines, badges, icons, stickers, or shapes.
9. **Spelling:** every word must be spelled exactly as written.

## 10. Content strategy — the weekly structure

**Goals, in order:** conversion (tours, memberships, bookings), awareness, brand voice, attracting members like the ones in our reviews.

**The week: 5 posts, Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM. Same image on Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn; network-specific caption.**

| Day | Post | Job | Photo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | **Wink** — one dry joke about working life | Reach, shares | Stock photo (green wash) or a Loft photo if the joke is about the space |
| Tue | **Testimonial** — a member's words, attributed | Trust | Loft people/lounge photo (green wash) |
| Wed | **Offer** — one product, its price, one CTA | Conversion | Loft photo of that product |
| Thu | **Question** — one question the community can answer in a comment | Engagement | Stock or Loft photo matching the question (green wash) |
| Fri, weeks A | **Member highlight** — name, what they do, why they're here | Community | Real photo of the member only |
| Fri, weeks B | **Soft sell** — a space or amenity photo with a light "come see it" | Awareness | Loft photo |

Rules:
- Wink: lands on the first read; the setup is something people actually do. Never a meme, never mean, never a price.
- Testimonial: from `inputs/testimonials.txt`, real words only, first name + initial. Never reuse one within 12 weeks.
- Offer: rotate products (Flex, Dedicated, Day Pass, Boardroom, Call Room, Virtual Office, Events). Never the same product two weeks running. Price always correct per the fact sheet.
- Question: answerable in one comment. Every other post may end with a small reply prompt where natural ("Which one are you?"), but never a sales CTA on Give posts.
- Member highlight: only with a real photo and the member's own answers from `inputs/members.txt`. **No member ready → run the Soft sell instead.** Nothing else changes.
- Two `green`-style posts per week: Tuesday (testimonial) and one of Monday/Thursday.
- Every post uses a real Loft 331 photo or a listed stock photo. Never AI-generated.

**Loft & Found — extra posts on top of the week. Tuesday and Thursday at 6:00 PM.**

Every second Wednesday of the month. Promotion starts the week after each event and runs to the next one. Facts (time, tickets, RSVP) come from `inputs/loft-and-found.txt`; photos only from the most recent event.

| Week | Tue 6 PM | Thu 6 PM |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (week after the event) | Recap: thank you, one moment, next date | Save the date |
| 2 | What it is, who comes | A photo from last time |
| 3 | Announce: date, time, how to get in | Why people come (an attendee's words) |
| 4 (event week) | Tomorrow | Wed 9 AM: Tonight · Thu 6 PM: Thank you |

Upcoming: 2026-10-14, 2026-11-11, 2026-12-09, 2027-01-13, 2027-02-10.

**Cycle:** two weeks at a time. Weeks alternate A / B. Nothing repeats within 8 weeks (checked against `posted-log.csv`).

**Example cycle (weeks A + B):**
- Week A: Mon Wink · Tue Testimonial · Wed Offer (Flex) · Thu Question · Fri Member highlight (or Soft sell)
- Week B: Mon Wink · Tue Testimonial · Wed Offer (Boardroom) · Thu Question · Fri Soft sell

## 11. Content calendar format — for export to Google Sheets

Always output the content calendar as **one single table** (not separate tables per week) so it can be exported to or pasted straight into Google Sheets.

Use **exactly these columns, in this order**:

| Week | Publish Date | Day & Time | Channel | Post Type | Bucket | Content Theme | Format | Key Message / Headline | Subline | Caption | Caption IG | Caption LinkedIn | Call to Action | Business Objective | Audience Pain Point | Photo File | Style | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Rules:

- **One row per content piece.**
- **Publish Date** in YYYY-MM-DD format. **Day & Time** like "Tue 9:00 AM".
- **Post Type / Bucket:** from the Section 10 tables (e.g. `Tip` / `Give`). The counts across the calendar must match the 5-3-2 rule.
- **Caption** is the Facebook caption. **Caption IG** and **Caption LinkedIn** are rewrites of the same post for those networks; same image on all three.
  - **Instagram:** hook in the first line, shorter than Facebook, no URL in the body (say "link in bio" on Sell posts), 8–12 hashtags on the last line from the pool plus #MonctonNB #coworkingspace #remotework #workfromhome #dieppe #riverview.
  - **LinkedIn:** slightly more professional, full sentences, URL is fine, no emoji, 3 hashtags max. Wink posts become the same idea told straight (an observation, not a joke).
- **Subline:** one short supporting line for the graphic, maximum 2 lines of 55 characters each (about 100 characters total). Not the caption.
- **Photo File:** the exact file name from Section 8 — a Loft 331 photo or a listed stock photo. `loft_gradient.png` only as a last resort. Never an invented name. Never "AI-generated".
- **Style:** `photo` or `green`. **Exactly 2 posts per week are `green`** (the brand-green wash over the photo); every other post is `photo` (neutral dark gradient). Spread the two green posts across the week, never back to back. Layout, logo, and text positions are identical in both styles; only the overlay changes.
- **Status:** always start as "Draft".
- **No merged cells, no line breaks inside cells**, and no extra notes inside the table. Put notes below the table.
- After the table, remind the user: **"To save this to Google Sheets, use Export to Sheets (if shown), or copy the table and paste it into cell A1 of a new Google Sheet."**

Keep headlines within 2 lines of about 26 characters and sublines within 2 lines of about 55 characters so they fit the template.
